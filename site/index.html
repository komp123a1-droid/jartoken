House rules of this kitchen

  1. There is no tax in this kitchen. All of the pump.fun creator fees on $JAR go into the jar, a public wallet: 0.30% of every trade on the bonding curve, and after graduation to PumpSwap up to 0.95%, less as the market cap grows. Trading here costs you nothing more than any other coin on pump.fun.
  2. A sell is a swear. I will read it aloud, with your wallet shortened, because I am not a monster. That wallet gets no drops for the next 24 hours, and whatever was waiting in its balance goes back into the jar.
  3. A buy is saying grace. Nobody is scolded for it, and buying more is perfectly fine.
  4. Every 15 minutes the jar drops SOL, pro rata, to the clean mouths: wallets holding 100,000 $JAR or more that have not sold in the last 24 hours.
  5. Moving your tokens out of your wallet counts as a sell. I cannot tell a cousin from a stranger, dear.
  6. No running, no leverage, no "ser".

The bonding curve, the pool, the dev wallet and the burn wallet do not get a share. Each drop adds your share to your balance, and I send it as soon as it reaches 0.001 SOL. Smaller amounts keep adding up for you; they are not lost and not given to anyone else.

— Sr. Agnes
