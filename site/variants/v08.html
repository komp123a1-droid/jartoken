Stop 02 · Language

Sister Agnes and the swear bot

Labels, in order of offence

Masking tape, marker, a public wallet. Ongoing.

Each strip of tape is a wallet that sold today. The bot reads it aloud the moment it happens, and that wallet forfeits its share until the next collection.

Buying more is fine. Moving tokens to another wallet counts as a sell. I do not make exceptions for “my other wallet,” dear.

    Stop 03 · The collection

    Performed nightly

    Midnight, UTC

    Duration: one batch of transfers, about twenty to a transaction.

    --:--:--until the jar is emptied

    At 00:00 UTC the jar is emptied. Every wallet holding at least 100,000 $JAR that did not sell today receives a share, pro rata. Anything under 0.001 SOL waits for tomorrow.

    The pool, the bonding curve, the burn address and my own wallet are not invited. The jar does not round up. Neither do I.

    — Sr. Agnes
